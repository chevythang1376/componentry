Motion feel

Scroll-driven motion is the one thing you cannot examine in place — by the time you have seen it, it has finished — and you certainly cannot compare two settings of it by scrolling twice. So every variant below is built through the real export path and then held at the same instant by the scrubber at the foot of the page.

Nothing here is a mock-up of the effect. The panels are the shipped markup and the shipped stylesheet; the animation ranges and the keyframe values are read back out of that stylesheet at run time, and the only thing this page supplies is a clock. Change a keyframe in the component and this page follows it.

Kinetic Text Reveal — stagger

How spread out the arrival is: 0 lands every word together, 10 is one at a time. Judge it around the middle of the scrub — the two ends look alike by definition.

Kinetic Text Reveal — by line, word and letter

One statement, one stagger, split three ways. Letter suits a short statement; on a long one it is a great deal of movement at once, and the pieces are small enough that the blur reads as fog rather than as focus.

Split Reveal — slats

More slats is a finer opening. Because the stagger is a share of the reveal rather than a gap between neighbours, adding slats makes the opening finer without making it longer — which is what you want, and what it did not do before.

Split Reveal — stagger and order

Seven slats throughout. One after another reads as a sweep, from the middle out as an opening.

45% 0% is before anything has arrived; 100% is fully settled. Everything worth judging happens between 25 and 75.
