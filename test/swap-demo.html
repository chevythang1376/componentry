Light to dark, on scroll

Scroll. None of these blocks has been told to swap — the project's Colour scheme is set to Light, swapping to dark on scroll, and every block following the project comes with it. They all read the same page scroll position, so they change together rather than one at a time.

The ground fades and the text steps once, near the middle. Fading both would pass through grey text on a grey ground, which is unreadable at roughly 1:1.

It crosses over about halfway down the page — that position follows the page, so halfway means halfway whatever this one turns out to be. How long it takes does not: the transition is about two thirds of a screen of scrolling on any page, which is what keeps it a change you watch rather than a colour that drifts.
