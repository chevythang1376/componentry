Scroll ranges that finish

A reveal driven by view() only finishes if its range is actually reachable. An element taller than the viewport, or the last thing on a page, can stop part-way through its entry phase and never come back — leaving text permanently half-faded and a cover permanently half-shut. Nothing else in the suite catches this: the degradation harness removes scroll timelines altogether, which is the case that already works. This one drives a real scroller.
