Colour scheme

A dark scheme has to be readable on every block, not most of them — and the swap has to move the ground and the text at different rates, because a light-to-dark crossfade of both is unreadable at its own midpoint by definition: interpolate the ground white-to-black and the text black-to-white and they meet at grey on grey, around 1:1. So the ground fades and the text steps, once, at the point where the worst moment of the whole transition is least bad — a point this page recomputes rather than takes on trust.
